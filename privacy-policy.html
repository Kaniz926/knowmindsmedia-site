Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KnowMinds Media ("we", "us", "our"), located at 3rd floor, Sehnaz House, Halidpadia, Dewligrameshwar, Jagatsinghpur, Odisha 754103, India, collects, uses and protects information when you visit knowmindsmedia.com or use our services, including our WhatsApp messaging and automation services.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, business name, email address, phone number and any message you send us.
  • Messaging data: when you interact with a business that uses our WhatsApp automation, we process your phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and the content of messages exchanged, on behalf of that business.
  • Technical data: basic information such as browser type, device and pages visited, used to keep the website working.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and improve our services.
  • To send automated replies, reminders and service messages on behalf of our business clients.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing of information

We share information only with service providers needed to run our services (for example Meta Platforms for WhatsApp Business messaging, hosting and email providers), with the business you are communicating with, or when required by law.

4. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or stop receiving messages by replying STOP on WhatsApp or emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Data deletion requests

To request deletion of your data, email hello@knowmindsmedia.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and the phone number or email linked to your data.

8. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
