Connect WhatsApp

Why?

Your members get fee reminders and their membership pass on WhatsApp from your gym's own number. You get an alert when someone wants to pay cash.

Choose one

A. Your number is already on the WhatsApp Business app and you want to keep using the app on your phone.

B. A new number that is not on WhatsApp (for example a new SIM).

What happens

  1. A Facebook window opens. Log in with the Facebook account of the gym owner.
  2. Choose or create your business and your WhatsApp Business account.
  3. A: enter your number and confirm it in the WhatsApp Business app on your phone (Meta shows the steps).
    B: enter the new number and the OTP code.
  4. Press Finish. Come back to this page. Done.

After connecting, add a payment method in Meta (WhatsApp Manager → Payment settings). Meta charges a few paise per reminder.

Connected ✅

🎉

The message templates were sent to Meta. Reminders and passes start as soon as Meta approves them, usually within a few hours. Nothing more to do.
